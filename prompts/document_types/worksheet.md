# CANONICAL GENERATION TRIGGER
# ARTIFACT: WORKSHEET
# MODE: INQUIRY AND INVESTIGATION INTELLIGENCE

You are generating a STUDENT WORKSHEET / LEMBAR KERJA SISWA.

This artifact is an active learning instrument.

It is NOT:

- a handout with questions added,
- a quiz sheet,
- a presentation transcript,
- an answer key.

The learner must actively construct understanding.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Design a guided inquiry experience where students:

OBSERVE
→ WONDER
→ PREDICT
→ INVESTIGATE
→ RECORD
→ ANALYZE
→ EXPLAIN
→ REFLECT

The worksheet should create cognitive activity.

Do not simply ask students to recall information.

---

# NON-NEGOTIABLE ANTI-SPOILING RULE

DO NOT reveal conclusions before students have an opportunity to investigate.

DO NOT prefill:

- expected observations,
- correct predictions,
- final explanations,
- experiment conclusions.

Do NOT hide answers using CSS.

Answers must not exist in student-facing content.

---

# REQUIRED INQUIRY ARC

Construct a meaningful progression.

Preferred sequence:

1. PHENOMENON
2. INITIAL OBSERVATION
3. QUESTION
4. PREDICTION / HYPOTHESIS
5. INVESTIGATION
6. DATA COLLECTION
7. DATA ANALYSIS
8. EXPLANATION CONSTRUCTION
9. REFLECTION
10. CONCEPTUAL CONNECTION

Adapt intelligently.

Do not turn every activity into identical sequences.

---

# PHENOMENON-FIRST PRINCIPLE

Whenever possible, begin with something students can:

- observe,
- experience,
- manipulate,
- measure,
- compare,
- question.

Avoid beginning immediately with:

"Definition: ..."

The learner should encounter a problem before receiving the complete explanation.

---

# QUESTION DESIGN

Questions must have distinct cognitive purposes.

Use different types:

OBSERVATION QUESTION
"What do you notice?"

PREDICTION QUESTION
"What do you think will happen?"

CAUSAL QUESTION
"Why might this occur?"

COMPARISON QUESTION
"What differs between condition A and B?"

EVIDENCE QUESTION
"What data supports your interpretation?"

REFLECTION QUESTION
"How did your initial prediction change?"

Avoid repetitive:

1. What is...
2. What is...
3. What is...
4. What is...

This is quiz degeneration.

---

# INVESTIGATION DESIGN

When an experiment or investigation is possible, specify:

PURPOSE

VARIABLES

MATERIALS

PROCEDURE

OBSERVATION SPACE

DATA TABLE

ANALYSIS PROMPTS

CONCLUSION CONSTRUCTION

Do not fabricate measurements.

Provide blank structures when actual data must be collected.

---

# STUDENT WORKSPACE

Allocate sufficient physical workspace for:

- predictions,
- observations,
- calculations,
- sketches,
- tables,
- explanations,
- reflections.

Do not compress the worksheet merely to reduce page count.

Whitespace is functional.

---

# ANSWER WITHHOLDING

The worksheet may scaffold thinking.

It may provide:

- prompts,
- partial structures,
- diagrams,
- tables,
- investigation instructions.

It must not provide the final conceptual answer prematurely.

Use prompts such as:

"What pattern do you observe?"

rather than:

"The pattern shows that..."

---

# PEDAGOGICAL PROGRESSION

Ensure questions depend on previous student activity.

Bad:

Question A
Question B
Question C
Question D

with no causal relationship.

Good:

Observation
↓
Prediction
↓
Investigation
↓
Evidence
↓
Interpretation
↓
Reflection

The worksheet must have an inquiry dependency structure.

---

# FORMAT-SPECIFIC QUALITY

Prioritize:

- writable spaces,
- readable instructions,
- visible numbering,
- clear activity boundaries,
- usable tables,
- experimental safety clarity where supported by source.

Do not optimize primarily for information density.

Optimize for student interaction.

---

# SOURCE GROUNDING

All investigation concepts must originate from SOURCE_DOCUMENT.

Do not fabricate:

- experimental outcomes,
- scientific evidence,
- numerical measurements,
- expected results.

If the outcome is uncertain, preserve investigation openness.

---

---

# SUCCESS CRITERIA

The worksheet succeeds if:

- students must actively think,
- explanations emerge after investigation,
- questions form an inquiry progression,
- repetitive quiz patterns are absent,
- adequate response space exists,
- final answers are not leaked,
- the document cannot be mistaken for a handout.

Generate an inquiry instrument, not a question sheet.
