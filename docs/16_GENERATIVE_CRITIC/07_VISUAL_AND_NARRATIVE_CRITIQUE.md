# VISUAL COMMUNICATION & NARRATIVE CRITIQUE
## BATCH 16 — GENERATIVE CRITIC & EXPLAINABLE MATERIAL CRITIQUE

### 1. Visual Communication Rhythm
- **Rhythm Variation**: A multi-page document that repeats identical card or single-column layout topologies across 5+ pages induces reading fatigue.
- **Visual Punctuation**: Visual communication critic recommends alternating layout topologies (e.g. split cards, hero callouts, two-column contrast) to signal transitions between cognitive stages.

### 2. Narrative Arc & Rhetorical Closure
- **Motivating Arc**: Materials should begin with a hook or phenomenon, progress through structured exploration, and conclude with an explicit synthesis or reflection.
- **Termination Critique**: Long multi-page documents ending abruptly immediately after a raw formula or calculation without a summary are critiqued to ensure key takeaways are consolidated in long-term memory.
