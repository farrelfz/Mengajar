# FALSE POSITIVE SAFEGUARDS & OVER-CRITICISM PROTECTION
## BATCH 16 — GENERATIVE CRITIC & EXPLAINABLE MATERIAL CRITIQUE

### 1. The Over-Criticism Risk
Generative critics can easily become hyper-critical by flagging intentional minimalism or valid alternative pedagogies as deficiencies.

### 2. Safeguards Built into Batch 16
1. **Intentional Minimalism**: A clean, single-concept lesson with concise text and correct prerequisite sequence produces 0 high or critical severity findings.
2. **Confidence Calibration**: Critics attach `CritiqueConfidence` to findings and skip critique when evidence is insufficient rather than guessing.
3. **Domain Relevance Filtering**: Domain-specific critics (e.g. `ScientificRigorCritic`) gracefully skip general educational materials where scientific claim analysis is inapplicable.
