# SCIENTIFIC RIGOR & AUDIENCE SUITABILITY CRITIQUE
## BATCH 16 — GENERATIVE CRITIC & EXPLAINABLE MATERIAL CRITIQUE

### 1. Scientific Rigor & Causal Integrity
- **Claim-Evidence Alignment**: Statements that equate statistical correlation with proven causation are identified and flagged as `HIGH` severity.
- **Rigor Guidance**: The critic recommends explicitly distinguishing observational correlation from controlled experimental causation.

### 2. Audience Prerequisite Adaptation
- **Grade-Level Appropriateness**: When targeting high school audiences, introducing tertiary or graduate-level formalisms (e.g. Lagrangian mechanics, Hamiltonian operators, metric tensors) without preparatory high school scaffolding triggers an audience prerequisite gap finding.
- **Remediation**: The critic recommends translating abstract formalisms into intuitive physical analogies.
