# CAPABILITY SELECTION CRITIQUE
## BATCH 16 — GENERATIVE CRITIC & EXPLAINABLE MATERIAL CRITIQUE

### 1. Semantic Appropriateness of Resolved Components
- **Background**: Batch 10 introduced 80+ capability templates and component families.
- **Role of the Critic**: Evaluates whether the chosen component family accurately reflects the instructional and relational semantics of the content.

### 2. Evaluated Anti-Patterns
- **Linear Process inside Comparison or Grid Blocks**: When a sequential multi-step procedure is rendered inside a parallel comparison or reference block, the visual hierarchy implies unordered parity. The critic diagnoses this as a structural mismatch and recommends re-resolving to `STEP_BLOCK` or `TIMELINE_ITEM`.
