## 3. Visual Diversity Findings

1. **Repetition Prevention**: The ecosystem demonstrates healthy diversity across **8 distinct visual topologies** (Pipelines, Matrices, Funnels, Hierarchies, Networks, Ladders, Equation Derivations, and Card Grids).
2. **Density Adaptation Verification**: Content rendered under `MINIMAL` density outputs clean summary cards; under `ANALYTICAL` or `DENSE_REFERENCE` it renders multi-column tables and parameter grids without visual clipping.
3. **Format Rhythms**: A4 Portrait establishes a natural top-to-bottom reading rhythm; A4 Landscape establishes 3-column horizontal comparisons; 16:9 Presentation establishes high-contrast presentation slides.
