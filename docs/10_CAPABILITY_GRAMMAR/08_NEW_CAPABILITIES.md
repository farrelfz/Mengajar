# 08 — Strategic New Capabilities

## Overview of 7 New Capabilities Introduced in Batch 8

Batch 8 introduced 7 high-value capabilities filling key structural and pedagogical gaps:

### 1. `universal.comparison_matrix`
- **Family**: `COMPARATIVE_REASONING`
- **Primary Intent**: `COMPARE`
- **Information Structure**: `MATRIX`
- **Visual Grammar**: `COMPARISON`
- **Description**: Domain-agnostic multi-item, multi-criterion tabular comparison card with badges and distinction highlights.

### 2. `universal.concept_hierarchy`
- **Family**: `CONCEPT_STRUCTURE`
- **Primary Intent**: `CLASSIFY`
- **Information Structure**: `HIERARCHY`
- **Visual Grammar**: `CONCEPT_MAP`
- **Description**: Hierarchical tree-structure concept breakdown with parent-child relationship levels.

### 3. `universal.evidence_chain`
- **Family**: `EVIDENCE_ANALYSIS`
- **Primary Intent**: `ARGUE`
- **Information Structure**: `EVIDENCE_CHAIN`
- **Visual Grammar**: `REASONING_FLOW`
- **Description**: Chain of premise, empirical evidence, inference warrant, and validated conclusion.

### 4. `pedagogy.question_progression`
- **Family**: `STEPWISE_REASONING`
- **Primary Intent**: `INVESTIGATE`
- **Information Structure**: `QUESTION_SET`
- **Visual Grammar**: `CHECKPOINT_CARD`
- **Description**: Scaffolding card displaying Bloom's taxonomy leveled questions (Recall -> Conceptual -> Analytical -> Synthesis).

### 5. `pedagogy.concept_checkpoint`
- **Family**: `ASSESSMENT_CHECKPOINT`
- **Primary Intent**: `ASSESS`
- **Information Structure**: `QUESTION_SET`
- **Visual Grammar**: `CHECKPOINT_CARD`
- **Description**: Formative assessment panel with diagnostic question, answer options, and misconception explanation.

### 6. `research.variable_relationship_map`
- **Family**: `RELATIONSHIP_MAPPING`
- **Primary Intent**: `RELATE`
- **Information Structure**: `MAPPING`
- **Visual Grammar**: `ANNOTATED_DIAGRAM`
- **Description**: Visual mapping of independent, dependent, moderating, and control variables with relationship directions.

### 7. `research.experiment_workflow`
- **Family**: `PROCESS_VISUALIZATION`
- **Primary Intent**: `SEQUENCE`
- **Information Structure**: `LINEAR_SEQUENCE`
- **Visual Grammar**: `PROCESS_FLOW`
- **Description**: Stepwise experimental protocol visualization with stage badges, procedural actions, and validation checkpoints.
