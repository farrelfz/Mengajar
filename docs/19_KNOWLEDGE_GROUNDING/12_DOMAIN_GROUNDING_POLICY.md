# DOMAIN GROUNDING POLICIES
## BATCH 19 — KNOWLEDGE GROUNDING & EVIDENCE INTELLIGENCE

### 1. Policy Registry (`DomainGroundingPolicyRegistry`)
Centralized domain policies:
- **Physics**: Strict grounding threshold ($\ge 0.85$), mandatory for definitions, formulas, and causal relations.
- **Research Methodology**: Enforces procedural and validity grounding ($\ge 0.80$).
- **Education**: Scaffolding and constructivist theory grounding ($\ge 0.75$).
- **General / Presentation**: Flexible grounding allowance for rhetoric ($\ge 0.70$).
