# EXPLAINABILITY & GROUNDING TRACE
## BATCH 19 — KNOWLEDGE GROUNDING & EVIDENCE INTELLIGENCE

### 1. Transparent Diagnostic Trace
Every Grounding Report answers:
1. What claims were extracted and analyzed?
2. What evidence was retrieved and ranked?
3. Why was specific evidence selected?
4. What source authorities were used?
5. Is the claim fully, partially, or un-grounded?
6. Are there contradictions?
7. What are the recommended remediation actions?
