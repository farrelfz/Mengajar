# 07 — Duration & Pacing Intelligence

## Time Budget Trajectory Modeling

`PacingPolicy` paces learning journeys according to `InstructionalTimeBudget`:

| Duration | Pacing Mode | Stages Included | Pedagogical Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **15 Minutes** | Rapid Briefing | 4 stages (`HOOK`, `FORMALIZATION`, `WORKED_EXAMPLE`, `SUMMARY`) | Immediate core concept delivery & visual hook. |
| **45 Minutes** | Standard Lesson | 7–8 stages (`HOOK`, `INTUITION`, `MISCONCEPTION`, `FORMALIZATION`, `EXAMPLE`, `PRACTICE`, `SUMMARY`) | Standard classroom progression with guided exercises. |
| **90 Minutes** | Deep Mastery | 10–12 stages (`HOOK`, `INTUITION`, `MISCONCEPTION`, `FORMALIZATION`, `DERIVATION`, `EXAMPLES`, `GUIDED_PRACTICE`, `INDEPENDENT_PRACTICE`, `CHALLENGE`, `REFLECTION`, `ASSESSMENT`) | Multi-variable problems, independent practice, and reflection rubrics. |
