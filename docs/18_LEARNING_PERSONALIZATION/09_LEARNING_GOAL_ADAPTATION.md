# LEARNING GOAL ADAPTATION
## BATCH 18 — LEARNING PERSONALIZATION & ADAPTIVE PEDAGOGICAL PROFILE SYSTEM

### 1. Goal-Driven Orchestration
Different goals on the same knowledge level yield distinct pedagogical journeys:
- `UNDERSTAND`: Prioritizes conceptual diagrams, analogies, and intuition.
- `PRACTICE`: Prioritizes progressive worked examples and self-check questions.
- `PREPARE_FOR_EXAM`: Focuses on formula breakdown, common pitfalls, and time-bounded practice.
- `CONDUCT_RESEARCH`: Focuses on research gap identification, hypothesis mapping, and variable controls.
- `TEACH_OTHERS`: Focuses on pedagogical explanation structures and misconception diagnostics.
