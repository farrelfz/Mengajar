# CROSS-ARTIFACT DIFFERENTIATION MATRIX & COLLAPSE DETECTION CONTRACT

This matrix establishes non-negotiable architectural differentiation across the four supported document artifacts.
High knowledge overlap from the source manifest is explicitly permitted, but **structural, cognitive, and pedagogical transformation divergence must remain distinct**.

---

## 1. CROSS-ARTIFACT DIFFERENTIATION MATRIX

| Dimension | PRESENTATION | HANDOUT | WORKSHEET (LKS) | SCIENTIFIC DOCUMENT (KTI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Purpose** | Orchestrate live visual teaching & cognitive moments | Comprehensive independent reading & conceptual study | Scaffold guided active discovery & scientific inquiry | Defend claims with empirical/literature evidence |
| **Audience Mode** | Synchronous viewing + speaker-guided listening | Asynchronous, self-paced, autonomous reading | Active student investigation & evidence recording | Critical scientific evaluation, verification & peer audit |
| **Information Density** | Low to Medium (0.20 – 0.50) | High (0.60 – 0.85) | Medium-Low (0.35 – 0.65) | Maximum / Highly Formal (0.75 – 0.95) |
| **Knowledge Compression** | High (core beats, visual grammar, progressive disclosure) | Moderate (contextual continuity, full prose, definitions) | High selective (targets inquiry variables; withholds outcomes) | High formal (evidence-structured, condensed method & data) |
| **Narrative Structure** | Dramatic / Didactic Narrative Arc (Hook → Tension → Resolution) | Hierarchical / Conceptual Taxonomy (Context → Theory → Application) | Inductive Inquiry Arc (Phenomenon → Prediction → Investigation → Reflection) | Deductive Formal BAB Hierarchy (Problem → Literature → Method → Discussion) |
| **Interaction Mode** | Attention directed by visual hierarchy & speaker cues | Linear & non-linear reading, referencing, marginal reflection | Physical handwriting, diagramming, measuring, calculating | Formal evaluation of claims, methodology, and limitations |
| **Explanatory Depth** | Progressive beats (omit micro-derivations for live delivery) | Deep 5-layer explanatory architecture (Intuition → Mechanism → App) | Scaffolded prompts; conclusions withhold until investigation | Rigorous claim-evidence-reasoning boundaries |
| **Visual Role** | Cognitive focal point, schema organizer, spatial relationship | Supportive diagrams, worked interpretation figures, reference tables | Functional blank data tables, apparatus setup, sketch workspaces | Formal data figures, schematics, statistical plots with captions |
| **Evidence Requirement** | Low-Moderate (illustrations, qualitative demonstrations) | Moderate (worked examples, illustrative proofs, case studies) | High student-generated (raw observation, measured values) | Absolute (verifiable citation links, direct empirical data) |
| **Knowledge Withholding** | Low (information disclosed sequentially per narrative) | Minimal (all foundational knowledge provided for self-study) | **STRICT (Never reveal outcomes, hypotheses, or answers pre-test)** | Zero (all evidence, caveats, and uncertainties stated upfront) |
| **Student Agency** | Perceptual & interpretative engagement | Comprehension, synthesis & self-directed revision | **Primary investigator, predictor, and conclusion synthesizer** | Independent evaluator of validity, method, and conclusions |
| **Uncertainty Handling** | Filtered to essential scope; high-level caveats only | Contextual caveats and common misconception callouts | Open-ended investigation variables and error reflections | **Epistemic boundary classification (KNOWN, INFERRED, UNCERTAIN)** |
| **Primary Failure Mode** | Handout Collapse (walls of dense text projected on slide) | Slide Fragmentation (chopped bullet points without continuity) | Quiz Collapse (repetitive recall questions; leaked answers) | Essay Collapse (unsupported claims, generic narrative rhetoric) |
| **Success Criterion** | Audience cognitive load preserved; narrative continuity clear | Reader achieves mastery independently without a live teacher | Student discovers scientific principles through guided inquiry | Document withstands forensic evidentiary and methodological scrutiny |

## 2. FORBIDDEN ARTIFACT COLLAPSE SIGNALS

Automated blueprint validators and LLM generators must immediately flag and reject the following structural collapse anti-patterns:

### A. PRESENTATION → HANDOUT COLLAPSE
- **Signal 1:** Slide blueprint contains paragraphs exceeding 3 sentences or lacks `VISUAL_GRAMMAR`.
- **Signal 2:** Cognitive load target consistently exceeds 0.65 across consecutive beats.
- **Signal 3:** Absence of `PROGRESSIVE_DISCLOSURE_PLAN` or `WHY_THIS_MUST_BE_A_SLIDE` justification.
- **Signal 4:** Slides serve as an exhaustive transcript rather than visual focal anchors for speech.

### B. HANDOUT → PRESENTATION FRAGMENTATION
- **Signal 1:** Handout sections are fragmented into bullet points lacking explanatory prose continuity.
- **Signal 2:** Omission of explanatory layers (`LAYER_1_INTUITION`, `LAYER_2_FORMAL_EXPLANATION`, `LAYER_3_MECHANISM`).
- **Signal 3:** Sections lack transitional glue (`TRANSITION_FROM_PREVIOUS`, `TRANSITION_TO_NEXT`).
- **Signal 4:** Definitions presented without contextual mechanism or application grounding.

### C. WORKSHEET → QUIZ COLLAPSE
- **Signal 1:** Sequence degenerates into repetitive `QUESTION_TAXONOMY: OBSERVATIONAL` or multiple-choice items.
- **Signal 2:** Complete absence of investigative stages (`PHENOMENON`, `INVESTIGATION`, `DATA_COLLECTION`).
- **Signal 3:** Absence of dedicated `WORKSPACE_REQUIREMENT` (forcing purely verbal recall).
- **Signal 4:** Inquiry dependency is broken (questions can be answered without preceding investigation).

### D. WORKSHEET → ANSWER LEAKAGE
- **Signal 1:** `WITHHOLD_EXPLANATION: false` or `ANSWER_LEAK_RISK: true` in observation/prediction activities.
- **Signal 2:** Expected experimental observations or conclusions are pre-filled in student instructions.
- **Signal 3:** Explanatory text contains the discovery target before the student collects data.
- **Signal 4:** Visual captions or diagrams reveal the phenomenon's underlying cause prematurely.

### E. SCIENTIFIC DOCUMENT → NARRATIVE ESSAY COLLAPSE
- **Signal 1:** Claims made without explicit `EVIDENCE_TYPE`, `EVIDENCE_DIRECTNESS`, or `SOURCE_TRACEABILITY`.
- **Signal 2:** `UNCERTAINTY_STATE` is omitted or unverified assumptions are upgraded to empirical facts.
- **Signal 3:** Causal assertions made using inferential or secondary evidence without justification.
- **Signal 4:** Total absence of `LIMITATION` disclosures or boundary conditions.
